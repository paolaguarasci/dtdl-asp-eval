Symbolic Analysis and LLM-Guided Debugging of Digital Twin Models with ASP Chef and DTDL

Mario Alviano · Paola Guarasci

Department of Mathematics and Computer Science (DeMaCS), University of Calabria, Italy

Abstract

DTDL (Digital Twins Definition Language) provides no mechanism for logical reasoning or constraint checking over digital twin models. We integrate DTDL with ASP Chef, a web-based Answer Set Programming (ASP) platform, via a structured DTDL-to-ASP mapping and three dedicated operations: @DTDL/Parse for fact generation, @DTDL/Analysis for structural metrics, and @DTDL/Debug for symbolic validation with LLM-guided repair. The key design decision is that error detection is symbolic and deterministic within the implemented set of constraint classes; a language model is invoked only after the ASP layer has produced a concrete, grounded diagnostic, keeping the correctness boundary within the symbolic layer. We illustrate the framework on two agricultural use cases and report a proof-of-concept assessment on 99 diagnostics spanning 21 error classes across four domains.

Contributions

  • A structured, deterministic mapping from DTDL constructs to ASP facts, making digital twin models amenable to declarative reasoning.
  • Three ASP Chef operations — @DTDL/Parse, @DTDL/Analysis, @DTDL/Debug — covering fact generation, structural metrics, and symbolic validation.
  • An architecture where the LLM is invoked only after a sound symbolic diagnostic, keeping detection deterministic and the correctness boundary in the symbolic layer.
  • A reproducible proof-of-concept evaluation (99 diagnostics, 21 error classes, 4 domains) with a grounded vs. fair-ablation comparison and independent multi-judge reliability analysis.
